Haze alerts

Get a notification when a region's 24-hr PSI reaches your threshold.

Alerts are checked each time the app refreshes (every 5 minutes while it is open). Some Android phones also check in the background after the app is installed, but browsers don't guarantee that, so keep the app open for the most reliable alerts.

Add to Home Screen

On iPhone or iPad: tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen. Alerts only work when the app is opened from the Home Screen icon.
